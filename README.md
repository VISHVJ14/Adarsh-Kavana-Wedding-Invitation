# Adarsh & Kavana • Luxury Digital Wedding Invitation Website

A luxury, cinematic, interactive digital wedding invitation website crafted for **Adarsh & Kavana** celebrating their traditional Kannada Hindu wedding at **Sri Shubamasthu Kalyana Mantapa, Arasikere, Karnataka** on **December 3 & 4, 2026**.

## ✨ Features
- **Interactive 3D Envelope**: Realistic wax seal crack sound, 3D envelope flap opening, and emerging invitation card with couple monogram `A & K`.
- **Cinematic Vertical Scrolling Story**:
  - **Auspicious Divine Opening**: Lord Sri Ganesha gold medallion with Kannada invocations (`|| ಶ್ರೀ ಗಣೇಶಾಯ ನಮಃ ||`, `|| ಶ್ರೀ ಲಕ್ಷ್ಮೀ ವೆಂಕಟೇಶ್ವರ ಪ್ರಸನ್ನ ||`).
  - **The Couple**: Adarsh & Kavana in traditional Karnataka wedding silk attire with Vedic Mangalasutra vow.
  - **Ceremonial Folio**: Royal Mysore parchment invitation.
  - **Celebration Details**: Wedding Reception (Dec 3) & Dhanassu Lagna Muhurtham (Dec 4) with 1-click Google Calendar & Apple Calendar (.ics) export, and Google Maps venue navigation.
  - **Couple's Real Moments**: Dedicated photographic showcase featuring the couple's genuine candid moments.
- **Audio Experience**: Background music playing *"Aaj Ibaadat"* (*Bajirao Mastani*) with live equalizer animations and Web Audio API synthesized temple bells.
- **Floating Navigation Dock**: Pinned quick navigation dock with active scroll-spy tracking.
- **Falling Petals Animation**: Dynamic falling rose and Mysore jasmine petals.

## 🚀 How to Host on GitHub Pages
1. Go to your repository on GitHub: **Settings** > **Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment** > **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Select branch **`main`** and folder **`/(root)`**, then click **Save**.
4. In a few moments, your live invitation website will be accessible at:
   `https://<your-github-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/`
